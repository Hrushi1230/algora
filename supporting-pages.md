# Algora — Section 7: Supporting pages (5 pages)

Image-generation prompts for GPT-4o / "gpt image 2". Each prompt renders ONE complete, pixel-perfect web screen as a single **16:9 desktop** screenshot. Copy-paste one block at a time. These are the trust, help, and legal pages — calm, clear, well-structured, always on-brand.

**Brand recap (do not deviate):**
Algora — a gamified platform where CS students master algorithms through synchronized visualization, code, and plain-English explanation. Tagline: *"See the algorithm think."*
Light theme only. Paper background, ink text, one teal accent. Instrument Sans (headings/body) + JetBrains Mono (code, labels, stats).

**Shared visual system (paste inside every prompt):**
- Background warm off-white paper #F7F9F8; elevated cards pure white #FFFFFF; hairline borders #E4E9E7.
- Text near-black ink #0E1513 for headings, muted slate #5B6763 for secondary/body text.
- ONE accent: teal #0E9C86 (highlight #14B8A6) — used only for primary buttons, active/selected states, focus rings, links, status dots, and the logo glyph.
- NO dark backgrounds anywhere, NO black panels, NO purple, NO rainbow gradients, NO glowing blobs/orbs, NO stock photos of people, NO emojis, NO cartoon mascots. Flat solid colors, crisp edges, soft realistic light shadows only.
- Type: Instrument Sans for headlines (large, tight, geometric) and body; JetBrains Mono for labels, dates, statuses, breadcrumbs. Corners 12–16px, 1px hairline borders, pale-teal focus ring, generous whitespace, high contrast, WCAG-AA. Ultra high-fidelity, 4k, clean.

**Shared MARKETING CHROME convention (paste inside every prompt in this section):**
- A persistent TOP NAV (white, hairline bottom border, full width): left = "algora" wordmark + small teal two-node graph glyph; center = mono nav links "Product", "Learning Paths", "Pricing", "About", "Help" (Help shown active where relevant); right = a ghost "Log in" link and a solid teal "Get started" button.
- A slim FOOTER (paper background, hairline top border): the "algora" wordmark, a short slate tagline, four compact mono link columns (Product / Learn / Company / Legal), and a bottom mono line "© 2026 Algora · See the algorithm think." Legal links (Privacy, Terms, Status) live here.
- The MAIN CONTENT sits centered on paper background between the nav and footer, in a comfortable reading measure.
